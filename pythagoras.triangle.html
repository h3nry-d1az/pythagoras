Triangle described by three points in \(\mathbf R^2\).

First of the points of the triangle; denoted by \(\rm A\).

Second of the points of the triangle; denoted by \(\rm B\).

Third of the points of the triangle; denoted by \(\rm C\).

property a: float¶

Length of the side opposite to \(\rm A\).

Angle that corresponds to the vertex \(\rm A\), i.e., \(\alpha = \min\{\angle BAC, \angle CAB\}.\)

Length of the side opposite to \(\rm B\).
